# User Guide: Student Management App

## 📝 Introduction
Welcome to the Student Management App! This platform helps you organize student, Give Quizes and Assighments to them and add courses. 

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### System Requirements
* **Browsers:** Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari, or Microsoft Edge.
* **Devices:** Website is responsive to Desktop, tablet and phone screens

### Sign Up
1. Go to the application URL in your browser.
2. Click on Sign Up Button.
3. Enter Your Details And Click on Sign UP.
4. Once Sign up you will be redirected to /Students/your_id

### Logging In
1. Go to the application URL in your browser.
2. Type in your Name and Reg#.
3. Click the **Log In** button to open your dashboard.

### Admin
Admin will enter secret key in url to access admin panel
```Text
/Admin?secret=Pet123
```
---

## 🧭 Navigation
Once you log in, you will see the Navigation in header.
* **Home:** To go to home screen.
* **Courses:** To See you register courses or rigister for New Course
* **Assignments:** To see Assigments give to you.
* **Quizes:** To see Quizes.
---

## 👥 Admin Managing Students

### Adding New Course
1. Go to **/Admin?secret=** Enter you key In query Parameter.
2. Click on **Manage Courses** in Courses card.
3. Here You can Add or Delete cources.

### Give Assignment
1. Go to **/Admin?secret=** Enter you key In query Parameter.
2. Click on **Manage Assignment** in Assignment card.

---

### Give Quiz
1. Go to **/Admin?secret=** Enter you key In query Parameter.
2. Click on **Manage Quiz** in Quiz card.

## ❓ Troubleshooting & FAQs

#### Q: I cannot log in. What should I do?
* Double-check your spelling for both your Name and Reg#.
* Make sure your `Caps Lock` key is turned off.
* Inputs fields are case sensitive

#### Q: The page is not saving my updates. What is wrong?
* Check your internet connection to make sure you are still online.
* Refresh the webpage and try entering the information one more time.
